Our Collective Goal


The roommate chores assignment form is meant to ensure fairness within dividing chores in a shared living space. These chores are organized based on a weekly rotation and each roommate's availability. Once the form is filled out, we will have a Sunday debrief to finalize our assignments. Remember, if you skip your chore, the more you take on!
